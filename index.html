01   Definition

What is
Prompt Engineering?

Prompt engineering is the discipline of crafting, refining, and optimising inputs given to AI language models in order to produce more accurate, relevant, contextualised, and useful outputs.

Think of it as the interface between human intent and machine intelligence. The same AI model can produce radically different results depending entirely on how you phrase your request.

INPUT - THE PROMPT
Your instruction to the AI: the task, context, constraints, audience and format combined.
OUTPUT - THE RESPONSE
The AI's generated answer, shaped entirely by the quality and specificity of your prompt.
PROMPT ANATOMY
Explain  how electricity is generated in a thermal power plant  in simple terms  for a high school student.

02   Significance

Why Prompts Matter

A poorly written prompt forces AI to guess. A precisely written prompt delivers expert-level answers instantly.

PRECISION
Specific prompts yield targeted answers. Vague prompts force the AI to guess your intent and return shallow, generic content.
EFFICIENCY
Good prompts eliminate back-and-forth. Get the precise answer you need in one attempt instead of five follow-up clarifications.
CONTEXT CONTROL
Adding who, what, why, and for whom transforms a generic response into a personalised, audience-appropriate answer.
OUTPUT QUALITY
The same AI model gives drastically different results based solely on prompt phrasing. The model is constant - your prompt is the variable.
STRUCTURED CONTROL
Prompts let you dictate tone, format, depth, length, and style - turning AI into a fully customisable writing and reasoning tool.
AI COLLABORATION
Prompt engineering is the new literacy. It is the primary skill that separates effective AI users from those who get mediocre results.

ChatGPT Great question! Here is how it works, step by step:

Step 1 - Fuel Burns → Heat
Coal, natural gas, or oil is burned inside a massive boiler, generating extreme heat (over 500°C).

Step 2 - Water → Steam
The heat converts water into high-pressure steam inside pipes running through the boiler.

Step 3 - Steam → Turbine Spin
The pressurised steam blasts against turbine blades, spinning them at thousands of RPM - like a jet engine.

Step 4 - Turbine → Generator
The spinning turbine is connected to a generator. Magnets rotating inside copper coils create electricity through electromagnetic induction.

Step 5 - Transformer → Power Grid
Voltage is stepped up to 400,000V for efficient long-distance transmission via pylons and cables.

Step 6 - Substation → Your Home
Local substations reduce the voltage back to a safe 230V before it enters your home through the distribution network.

07   Analysis

Why the Specific
Prompt Succeeded

Four engineering decisions in the prompt produced a dramatically better result from the exact same AI model.

01
CONTEXT & SUBJECT CLARITY
We specified exactly which aspect of electricity to cover - thermal power plant generation and grid distribution - eliminating the AI's need to guess what we meant and directing it to the correct knowledge domain.
02
DEFINED AUDIENCE
Specifying "high school student" calibrated vocabulary, analogies, and complexity. The AI chose relatable comparisons (turbine like a jet engine) and avoided impenetrable jargon - perfectly matched to the reader.
03
SCOPE BOUNDARIES
Requesting the complete journey from fuel burning to home delivery set a clear start and end point. No wasted tangents about solar or nuclear power - just the defined process, fully covered.
04
IMPLICIT FORMAT SIGNAL
"Explain... step by step... simple terms" implicitly requested a numbered, sequential, digestible structure. The AI delivered exactly that rather than a dense unbroken paragraph that no student would read.

08   Framework

Key Elements of a
Good Prompt

Every high-quality prompt is built from four essential components. Master these and you master AI communication.

01   TASK / ACTION
Start with a precise action verb that tells the AI exactly what to do. Vague verbs produce vague responses. Specific verbs unlock structured outputs.
Explain / Compare / Summarise / List / Write / Translate / Analyse
02   CONTEXT
Provide the situation, background, or domain. The AI cannot read your mind - give it enough information to anchor its reasoning in the right space.
...in a thermal power plant... / ...for a startup in 2024... / ...in Indian law...
03   AUDIENCE
Who is the response for? Define the reader's expertise, age, role, or background. This single variable controls vocabulary, tone, analogies, and technical depth.
...for a high school student / ...for a non-technical CEO / ...for an expert engineer
04   FORMAT / STRUCTURE
Specify how the output should look. Bullet points, numbered steps, a comparison table, a short paragraph, or a script. Structure guides both readability and completeness.
Use numbered steps / Format as a table / Keep under 200 words / Use subheadings
C·A·T·O
Context + Action + Target Audience + Output Format = A prompt that reliably produces professional-grade AI responses every time.
09   Best Practices

Prompt Writing
Tips & Best Practices

Be Specific, Not Vague
Replace "tell me about X" with "explain Y aspect of X for Z audience using N format." Every added detail narrows the AI's search space.
"Tell me about AI" → "Explain how transformer models work for a computer science undergrad"
Assign a Role
Prepend "Act as a [role]" to frame the AI's perspective, expertise, and tone. This single technique dramatically improves depth and accuracy.
"Act as a senior physics professor explaining to a curious 15-year-old..."
Specify Output Format
Ask for bullet points, numbered steps, a comparison table, markdown, JSON, or code. Structured output requests produce structured, readable results.
"Format your response as a numbered list with bold headings and one example each."
Use Few-Shot Examples
Show the AI an example of exactly what you want. "Like this: [example]" is often the single most powerful technique for controlling output style.
"Write 3 taglines. Like this: 'Power your world with clean energy.'"
Iterate & Refine
Treat prompting as an ongoing conversation. Use precise follow-up instructions: "Make step 3 more detailed", "Simplify the vocabulary", "Add an analogy."
"Good - now rewrite step 4 using a car engine analogy for clarity."
Set Explicit Constraints
Define what NOT to include. Negative constraints are as important as positive ones - they prevent the AI from padding, going off-topic, or over-complicating.
"No jargon. No equations. No more than 150 words. Avoid mentioning nuclear energy."
Use Chain-of-Thought
For complex problems, add "Think step by step before answering." This forces the model to reason systematically and dramatically reduces errors in logical tasks.
"Think step by step before answering: solve this thermodynamics problem..."
Test & Benchmark
Compare multiple prompt versions side by side. Evaluate on accuracy, completeness, tone, and length. Prompt engineering is a scientific process - measure, compare, improve.
Run the same query with 3 different phrasings and score which response is most useful.
